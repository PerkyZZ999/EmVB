# Schedule publishing

Since 1.2 you can choose when a page or theme part goes live, straight from the editor. EmVB uses
EmDash's own scheduling (EmDash 1.4 and 1.5), so EmDash publishes it at that time by itself, with
no browser open.

## Schedule a page

1. In the editor's top bar, press the calendar button beside **Publish** (named **Schedule**).
2. Pick the date and time. The dialog shows the time zone it uses: your browser's.
3. Press **Schedule**.

Unsaved changes are saved first. Until the time comes, visitors keep seeing what is live now; then
your saved changes publish. The time must be at least a minute from now.

While a page is scheduled, the top bar says when, for example "Publishes 14 Nov 2026, 09:00".

## Change or cancel it

Press the same button, now named **Change schedule**. Pick another time and press **Change
schedule**, or press **Cancel schedule** to keep the page as it is until you publish it yourself.

## Theme parts

Headers, footers, popups and other theme parts in the Theme Builder schedule the same way, from
the same button in their editor.
